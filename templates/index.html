MUMBAI LOCAL

Graph Lab

The suburban rail network as a graph: every station is a vertex, every track section is an edge. Discrete Mathematics, Module 6 (Graphs), with Module 3 (Relations) on the side.

Schematic map, not to scale. Stations between two fixed points are spaced evenly. Click a station to pick it, scroll to zoom, drag to pan.

Data: Wikipedia pages for the Western, Central, Harbour and Trans-Harbour lines of the Mumbai Suburban Railway (suburban stations only; no metro or monorail). The model ignores timetables and fast or slow trains, so it counts stops and interchanges, not minutes.
